The journey

What did we do?

We upgraded Ivan for longer travel and headed west from Cincinnati. Death Valley gave us immense empty landscapes, dark skies and our first growing list of places a minivan could not reach. We turned north through California, reconnected with the Pacific at Coos Bay and began the long southbound run that completed the remaining stretch of the U.S. Pacific Coast.

Highway 1 was not a straight line. Storms, water over the road and a major slide closure forced us inland to Pinnacles before we approached the break from the south. After Los Angeles, Spencer joined us for the route through St. George, Page and the Grand Canyon. Cassie and David then continued through New Mexico, West Texas and Big Bend before reaching Miami. Pacific to Atlantic: complete.

The turning point

What did it prove?

PCH-Ex proved that Ivan could support a journey measured in months—not weeks.

We learned how to pack for changing climates, sleep night after night, make hot meals wherever we stopped and keep moving through cold, storms, road closures and long distances. The soft-sided cooler, mattress topper, extra storage, Jackery 1000 and rechargeable fan made the system substantially better.

But every “4x4 only” sign clarified what came next. Ivan could take us across a continent; the future rig needed to carry the comfort, power, refrigeration and access that would move our adventures beyond the pavement.
