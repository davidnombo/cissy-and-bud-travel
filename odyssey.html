One very big loop

We leave Cincinnati and point west toward San Francisco. That is only the beginning.

From California we turn south to Los Angeles, then east across the country to Key West. We return north through Cincinnati and continue through Calgary, Fairbanks and Deadhorse before coming south through Seattle to San Francisco.

The directional route

  • West: Cincinnati to San Francisco
  • South: San Francisco to Los Angeles
  • East: Los Angeles to Key West
  • North: Florida through Cincinnati, Calgary and Fairbanks to Deadhorse
  • South: Alaska through Seattle to San Francisco

The route is directional, not turn-by-turn. Detours are the point. Reservations will be rare, weather will have a vote, and the places that surprise us may change everything.

PourHouse: our mobile basecamp

PourHouse is our Revel Sprinter and the latest version of a dream that began when we noticed overland rigs during the 2019 Prius trip. It carries our bed, bathroom, refrigerator, food and power, making it possible to immerse ourselves in travel while staying financially efficient.

David plans much of the route, finds wild camping and manages the van. Cassie navigates from the passenger seat, manages our social calendar and keeps us connected to our community. The dream—and the decision to keep moving—belongs to both of us.

Follow the long way

The journal is public because people in our lives kept asking us to share more. With The Transcontinental Odyssey upon us, there was no reason to wait.

We are not here to tell anyone how to live. We hope the journey creates a few useful questions: Am I happy? What else could I do? Which limits are real? Am I doing what is expected, or what I want?
