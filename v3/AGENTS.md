# V3 editorial ownership

The Gallery is personally curated by David. Do not add, replace, reorder, or remove Gallery photographs without his explicit prompt authorizing that Gallery change after his review. Album access, imports, and Field Note photo work do not authorize Gallery changes. Existing Gallery content remains until David asks to change it.

`content/gallery.json` is the sole Gallery selection. Never auto-populate it from album imports, Field Note pools, or asset-directory scans.

`content/field-note-photos.json` contains separate expedition-specific rotation pools for individual Field Notes. Each photo must belong to its expedition. Imports live under `assets/photos/field-notes/<expedition>/`. Keep photos local at runtime. Preserve original V2 and story source prose.
