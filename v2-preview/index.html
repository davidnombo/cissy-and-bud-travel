Frequently asked, occasionally answered

The questions everyone asks—usually about bathrooms.

Every answer below is a realistic mock draft. Cassie and David will replace, correct or remove it before launch.

Current question in rotation

How do you go to the bathroom?

Small-space travel inspires practical curiosity. We understand. These are the questions people ask once they realize the vehicle is also the bedroom, kitchen, office and weather shelter.

How do you go to the bathroom?

Mock answer: The honest answer is that it depends on the rig and where we are. Campgrounds, trailheads and businesses do a lot of the work. For everything else, we carry a compact emergency solution and follow leave-no-trace rules. The system is less dramatic than people hope.

Where do you sleep?

Mock answer: Inside the vehicle whenever we can. Each rig has had some version of a level sleeping platform, real bedding and ruthless storage underneath. A good night often begins with finding level ground before dark.

How do you shower?

Mock answer: Campgrounds, recreation centers, truck stops and the occasional hotel reset. On shorter stretches, a basin, warm water and a washcloth can carry more dignity than you might expect.

What happens when something breaks?

Mock answer: We diagnose what we can, improvise what we should and call a professional before confidence turns into expensive stupidity. Breakdowns are inconvenient, but they also become mile markers in the story.

How do you choose where to go next?

Mock answer: One anchor destination, a loose route and permission to change the plan. Weather, road closures, recommendations and plain curiosity usually improve the original itinerary.

Do Cassie and David ever get tired of each other?

Mock answer: Of course. The useful skills are saying so kindly, taking a walk and remembering that silence inside a vehicle is not automatically a crisis.

What do you do about laundry?

Mock answer: Laundromats become tiny civic centers: Wi-Fi, weather checks, grocery planning and one blissful hour when every piece of clothing is clean at once.

Which rig has been your favorite?

Mock answer: The next one when something breaks; the current one when camp is perfect; and Mighty V whenever we remember how little it took to get started.

About Us + Support Us

We are Cassie and David.

PourHouseLife is what happens when the route becomes the story.

Taking the detour together

Traveling at human speed.

We did not begin with the perfect rig or a master plan. We began by making enough room to sleep in a Prius V and discovering that the road did not care how elaborate our setup was.

Since then, each vehicle has changed the scale of what felt possible. PourHouseLife is our place to preserve the trips, the practical lessons, the disagreements, the accidental campsites and the reasons we keep choosing the long way.
