Frequently asked

FAQs

How do you go to the bathroom?

It depends on the rig and where we are. Campgrounds, trailheads, and businesses do a lot of the work. We also carry a compact emergency solution and follow leave-no-trace rules.

Where do you sleep?

Inside the vehicle whenever we can. Each rig has shaped a different version of the sleeping platform, bedding, storage, and search for level ground.
