About us

We no longer vacation.

A vacation is a break from routine. We shredded routine.

We’re Cassie and David. We expected to work well into our sixties. Instead, a summer off, a cancer diagnosis and an open road changed the direction of our lives.

We’re not here to prescribe a better life. We’re here to ask whether the life you have is the one you want.
