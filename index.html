Why we wander

We no longer vacation.

A vacation is a break from routine. We shredded routine.

We were business professionals with the usual responsibilities and headaches. Like most people, we expected to work well into our sixties. Then, a cancer diagnosis changed our horizon. We kept moving without a grand plan—and built freedom as we saw it.
