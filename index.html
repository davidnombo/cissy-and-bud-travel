About us · working draft

From a Prius
to the open road.

We're Cassie and David. Every vehicle gave us a little more freedom—and changed what “home for the night” could mean.

We started in a Prius V after our son invited us to see Utah's national parks. We drove from Cincinnati to Denver, picked him up at the airport and kept going to Las Vegas. Soon afterward, a twelve-day loop with Cassie's parents took us down the Washington coast, inland to Crater Lake and back to Seattle. We were hooked.

The Town & Country gave us a bed and more than 100 nights on the road. In April 2025, we moved into a Revel Sprinter that lets us stay off-grid for days at a time. Our style keeps evolving, but the goal stays simple: more freedom, more wonder and enough time to notice where we are.

This is the public working draft. Cassie's perspective and the complete backstory will be added through an interview before launch.

— Cassie and David
